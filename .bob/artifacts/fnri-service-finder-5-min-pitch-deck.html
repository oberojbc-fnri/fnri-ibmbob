Slide 4 of 7 2:00 – 2:45
The AI Upload Engine
Any agency can onboard in 15 minutes — no developer needed.
  • Agency staff visits the Upload page and types their agency name
  • Drags and drops their official Citizen Charter PDF
  • IBM watsonx reads the PDF and extracts all services automatically (~2 min)
  • Staff reviews the results on screen — edits anything if needed
  • Clicks "Publish" — the agency Service Finder goes live immediately
Why it works for every agency: RA 11032 mandates the exact same charter format — Client Steps → Agency Action → Fees → Processing Time → Person Responsible — so our AI model understands any agency's document out of the box.
Speaker note: "The standardised format is the key insight. Because every charter looks the same by law, the AI doesn't need to be retrained per agency. One model, any charter."
